What We Do

Building readiness infrastructure for future in-space servicing.

Antandros helps spacecraft teams become service-ready through standards-aligned readiness technologies and staged development toward non-contact inspection and servicing-readiness verification.

Readiness foundation

Customer-side readiness is the starting point.

Our work focuses first on customer-side readiness technologies, compatibility planning inputs, and documentation structures intended to support future inspection and servicing workflows.

This readiness-first approach is designed to reduce uncertainty and help teams evaluate future servicing pathways using evidence-driven criteria.

Core capabilities in development

Staged capabilities aligned to risk-aware progress.

Readiness technologies

Develop technologies and practices intended to help spacecraft prepare for future inspection, approach, and servicing scenarios.

Inspection and assessment support

Advance non-contact inspection outputs designed to support engineering assessment and servicing-readiness verification.

Standards alignment

Maintain standards-aligned traceability so readiness assumptions are documented and reviewable across stakeholders.

Interoperability planning

Support compatibility planning for cooperative spacecraft while avoiding universal compatibility claims.

Demonstration and validation

Use staged demonstration and validation steps to build confidence before future servicing support capabilities.

Future servicing support roadmap

Treat physical servicing support as a future capability in a staged roadmap, not a current operational service.

Public-safe positioning

Current focus: readiness and verification, not operational servicing.

Antandros is developing capabilities and candidate applications in LEO-first contexts with conservative claims and staged validation.

Future servicing support remains a roadmap direction that may expand only after readiness, inspection, and validation milestones.
