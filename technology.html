What We Do

Customer-side readiness technologies plus inspection-first servicing-readiness infrastructure.

Antandros positions customer-side readiness technologies as the foundation, paired with standards-aligned readiness and servicing infrastructure that supports inspection, assessment, and verification for cooperative spacecraft.

Architecture premise

Two sides of a service-ready ecosystem.

Customer-side readiness technologies are foundational: cooperative interface concepts intended to make spacecraft easier to inspect, assess, and verify as service-ready.

The infrastructure focus is readiness criteria, documentation, inspection and assessment support outputs, servicing-readiness verification, and a staged roadmap toward future servicing support.

Core functions

The early stack is designed around non-contact confidence-building.

Spacecraft readiness technologies

Define readiness features, inspectable references, and documentation inputs that support service-readiness planning and assessment.

Readiness criteria and documentation

Define readiness criteria, evidence expectations, and documentation that help teams evaluate service readiness with clear assumptions.

Inspection and assessment support

Plan non-contact inspection and assessment support outputs that inform customer-led engineering decisions.

Servicing-readiness verification

Trace inspection observations to readiness criteria and verification checkpoints, while keeping outputs assessment-support only.

Standards alignment

Align readiness technologies and service assumptions with emerging standards and interoperability expectations.

Staged validation roadmap

Use staged, risk-aware validation so readiness technologies can mature from early evidence to broader demonstration.

Responsible claims

Inspection-first does not overclaim diagnostics or operational servicing.

Antandros treats initial inspection outputs as assessment-support services. Visual or sensor data can support customer engineering review, but the confidence level depends on sensor suite, geometry, lighting, client documentation, and mission-specific validation.

Future servicing support remains a staged roadmap extension. Potential capabilities are roadmap-only and are not presented as current operational services.
