Standards

Standards-aligned readiness and interoperability planning.

Antandros uses standards-aligned methods to support compatibility planning, readiness criteria, and documentation quality across staged servicing pathways.

Why standards matter

Interoperability starts with shared readiness assumptions.

Service-ready spacecraft programs benefit from common readiness language, documented criteria, and repeatable review processes.

Antandros emphasizes staged, risk-aware development so teams can evaluate technical readiness with transparent evidence rather than broad compatibility claims.

Standards posture

Core standards-aligned practices in development.

Readiness criteria

Define and maintain readiness criteria that support inspection planning and servicing-readiness verification workflows.

Documentation discipline

Structure readiness documentation to reduce ambiguity, improve reviewability, and support staged capability progression.

Compatibility planning

Support interoperability planning for cooperative spacecraft without asserting guaranteed or universal compatibility outcomes.

Risk-aware development

Progression is tied to evidence.

Antandros uses staged development checkpoints intended to connect requirements, readiness criteria, and observed results.

This approach is designed to reduce custom engineering burden over time while preserving conservative public claims.
