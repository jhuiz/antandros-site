# Antandros public copy notes

## North star

Antandros supports space asset management through advanced in-orbit inspection and spacecraft-side serviceability components.

## Public framing

- Inspection informs asset-management decisions.
- Serviceability components make future servicing less custom, less expensive, and more worthwhile.
- Life-extension and decommissioning are future servicing outcomes.

## Use often

- space asset management
- advanced in-orbit inspection
- spacecraft-side serviceability components
- affordable in-orbit servicing
- commercially worthwhile servicing
- life-extension servicing
- responsible decommissioning
- reduce custom mission work
- satellite asset assessment
- future servicing support

## Avoid public overclaims

- current operational servicing
- flight-proven capability
- guaranteed compatibility
- universal standard
- current docking, refueling, towing, deorbit, or repair service
- full anomaly diagnosis
- NASA/SBIR/funding references unless legally cleared
