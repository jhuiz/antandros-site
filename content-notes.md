# Antandros content notes

## Public-safe source of truth

Current website copy follows a public-safe positioning:

- Antandros helps spacecraft become service-ready.
- Antandros is developing standards-aligned spacecraft readiness and servicing infrastructure.
- Customer-side readiness technologies are the foundation.
- Advanced non-contact inspection and servicing-readiness verification are staged capabilities in development.
- Future servicing support is a roadmap capability, not a current operational service.
- Development language should remain staged, risk-aware, and evidence-driven.

## Public-safe wording guardrails

Prefer language such as:

- developing
- designed to
- intended to
- roadmap
- future capability
- candidate application
- inspection and assessment support
- servicing-readiness verification
- standards-aligned
- staged development

Avoid unapproved claims such as:

- current operational servicing claims
- flight-proven claims
- guaranteed/universal compatibility claims
- full-diagnostic claims
- funding/program references not cleared for public release

## Content posture reminders

- Keep future servicing support framed as roadmap capability only.
- Keep inspection outputs framed as assessment-support data.
- Keep compatibility language tied to planning and interoperability, not guarantees.
- Keep LEO-first and staged validation framing where relevant.
