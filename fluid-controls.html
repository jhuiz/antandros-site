A developing product line within Aeterna Sidera

Fluid Controls — In Development

Our initial focus is normally closed, electrically actuated firing valves, developed toward an engineering-evaluation offering with characterized behavior and clear integration information.

Discuss an application

Initial offering

Hardware and the evidence to evaluate it.

Our propulsion development has highlighted the importance of understanding valve behavior under the conditions in which a system will actually operate. Flow capacity, pressure limits, switching response, leakage, and repeated operation need to be considered together.

The planned evaluation package would pair a defined hardware configuration with interface documentation, measured performance, test conditions, and explicit limitations. The objective is to help developers assess suitability without treating a nominal specification as evidence for every operating condition.

Development status: evaluation hardware is not yet available. Published content describes our intended scope, not demonstrated performance, qualification, or flight readiness.

Product direction

Components for independent integration.

The intended product line would be available independently of Aeterna propulsion systems, including to other propulsion developers. We welcome application discussions with spacecraft-fluid and test-system teams as well.

  • Valve configurations: defined service conditions and interfaces, with application suitability assessed against supporting evidence.
  • Optional integration equipment: matched drive electronics, harnesses, and interface accessories are being considered as part of the offering.
  • Evaluation documentation: configuration identification, measured results, handling guidance, and stated operating limitations.

These are development intentions. The initial configurations, available accessories, commercial terms, and delivery timing have not been established.

Applications and potential evaluation partners

What would make a valve useful to your system?

We welcome non-sensitive conversations about the operating conditions, integration constraints, and evidence your team needs to evaluate a valve. A brief description of the application and its principal selection challenges is a useful starting point; a complete requirements package is not necessary for an initial conversation.

These discussions inform development priorities and do not constitute a product reservation or a commitment to supply hardware.
