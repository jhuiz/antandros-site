In development

Fluid controls.

Aeterna is developing normally closed, electrically actuated firing valves in-house as part of our vertically integrated propulsion program.

The product line is intended for our own propulsion systems and for independent supply to other propulsion, spacecraft-fluid and test-system developers.

Initial product line

Firing valves.

Our valve development focuses on repeatable switching, low pressure loss and low leakage during repeated operation. Matched drive electronics, harnesses and interface accessories are also under consideration.

We plan to provide evaluation valves with measured performance, test conditions, operating limitations and interface documentation.

Evaluation hardware is not yet available.
