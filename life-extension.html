Life Extension

Future Life-Extension and Decommissioning Support

Antandros is building toward servicing pathways that help operators extend, reposition, or responsibly retire valuable space assets.

Lifecycle support

Life extension and decommissioning only make sense when the mission is worthwhile.

Future servicing outcomes depend on both asset condition and servicing economics. Antandros is developing the inspection capabilities and serviceability components needed to make those pathways more realistic.

Life extension

Keep valuable assets useful for longer.

Satellites can remain valuable beyond their original mission assumptions. Future life-extension servicing may support continued operations, mission adaptation, or asset preservation when the economics justify intervention.

Decommissioning

Responsible end-of-life operations need better tools.

As orbital activity grows, decommissioning becomes a space asset management problem. Operators may need future support for relocation, towing, or deorbit-related services when a spacecraft reaches the end of its useful mission.

How Antandros fits

Inspection informs the action. Serviceability changes the business case.

Assess before committing

Before deciding whether to extend, relocate, or decommission a spacecraft, operators need better information. Non-contact inspection can help characterize visible asset condition and servicing context.

Reduce custom mission work

Serviceability components can help future servicing providers evaluate spacecraft more efficiently and improve the economics of life-extension and decommissioning support.

Roadmap-only capability

Future support, not current operational service.

Antandros’ long-term roadmap may include future support areas such as life-extension servicing, relocation support, towing support, decommissioning support, prepared-spacecraft servicing compatibility, and ORU-related support.

These are future capability areas and should not be interpreted as current operational services.
