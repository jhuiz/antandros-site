Founder-led spacecraft propulsion development

Developing in-space chemical propulsion for spacecraft maneuvering.

Aeterna Sidera Inc. is developing an in-space bipropellant chemical-propulsion program, with an approximately 1 N-class head as the initial direction. The program treats propellant source/feed behavior and the head-end as one coupled engineering problem.

Current work is pre-hardware: application definition, coupled system analysis, numerical-method verification, interface development, and test planning.

Intended application domain

When maneuver time and acceleration matter.

Electric propulsion excels at efficient cumulative velocity change. Stored-gas systems can support simple, precise impulses. Aeterna is investigating chemical propulsion for mission events where response time and acceleration create value.

Candidate applications include rapid rephasing, time-constrained conjunction response, rendezvous, orbit correction, disposal, and contingency recovery. These are design-driving application cases—not claims of customer adoption or demonstrated mission performance.

  • Rapid rephasing
  • Conjunction response
  • Rendezvous
  • Orbit correction
  • Disposal
  • Contingency recovery

Development boundary

One coupled propulsion problem—from propellant source to nozzle exit.

The intended long-term product boundary runs from a propellant source/feed system through the head-end to the nozzle exit. Requirements and interfaces are being developed as a coupled problem. Exact components and internal architecture remain open engineering decisions.

Evidence-gated progression

Evidence before scale.

Aeterna advances a configuration only when its evidence supports the next question. Each stage supports only the analyzed or tested configuration, conditions, and stated purpose.

  1. 01

    Bootstrap engineering

    Current

    Define applications and maneuver events; evaluate coupled feasibility; establish numerical, interface, verification, facility, safety, and planning bases.

  2. 02

    Atmospheric head development

    Planned

    If authorized at the preceding gate, develop one evolving product-path head article with a dynamic two-propellant ground supply, then measure inlet demand and correlate the analytical model.

  3. 03

    Integrated one-gravity development

    Future

    Converge one product-path source/feed article with the developed head in an atmospheric source-to-plume configuration.

  4. 04

    Later maturation

    Future

    Vacuum or altitude operation, low-gravity acquisition, endurance, environment, qualification, acceptance, manufacturing, and flight-intent work remain separate evidence efforts.

Company and method

Founder-led, claim-limited development.

Aeterna Sidera is led by Jesus Huizar, Founder, CEO, and Chief Engineer. His background spans chemical engineering, spacecraft propulsion, multiphysics modeling, and experimental hardware, including a NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory internship and university propulsion research.

Technical decisions remain with the Founder and Chief Engineer. Specialists and independent reviewers are planned for later gates where their expertise or independence is required. Public claims are bounded to the configuration, conditions, and evidence actually established.

Spacecraft owners and primes retain vehicle requirements, installed-system qualification, launch and mission acceptance, and flight authority.
