Propulsion and fluid controls for spacecraft.

Aeterna Sidera is developing in-space bipropellant chemical propulsion and a fluid-control component line. Both programs begin with mission needs and advance through engineering and test evidence.

Propulsion · In development

When maneuver time and acceleration matter.

Our in-space bipropellant chemical-propulsion program considers the propellant source, feed system, and thruster head together. Candidate applications include rephasing, rendezvous, orbit correction, and disposal.

The program is in the engineering-definition stage, prior to hardware development. Its planned progression runs from controlled ground development toward integrated-subsystem testing and later maturation.

Fluid Controls · In development

Components defined by their operating conditions.

Our developing fluid-control line begins with electrically actuated, normally closed firing valves. The focus is characterizing flow, leakage, switching behavior, and repeated operation together with the fluid, pressure, temperature, and electrical conditions that affect them.

The intended offering is independent of Aeterna's propulsion systems. We welcome application discussions with propulsion, spacecraft-fluid, and test-system developers. Evaluation hardware is not yet available.

Company and method

Founder-led engineering.

Aeterna Sidera is led by Jesus Huizar, Founder, CEO, and Chief Engineer. He holds a B.S. in Chemical Engineering from UC Berkeley and an M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from UC San Diego. His experience spans spacecraft propulsion, multiphysics modeling, and experimental hardware, including a NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory internship and university propulsion research.

Technical direction is led by the Founder and Chief Engineer. The development plan includes future specialist participation and independent technical review.
