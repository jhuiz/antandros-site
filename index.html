Current work

A focused, solution-neutral industry study.

The study covers recurring challenges across spacecraft and payload requirements, supplier selection, integration, verification, production, procurement, quality, and acceptance.

We are seeking perspectives from people who directly own spacecraft or payload requirements, integration, verification, production, quality, procurement, supplier selection, or acceptance.

Participation

What participation means.

Participants may answer five short questions in writing or have a 15-minute conversation. Examples showing that an existing process works reliably are equally useful.

We will not identify or quote respondents publicly without permission. Please do not share classified, export-controlled, proprietary, or procurement-sensitive information.
