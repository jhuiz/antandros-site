The problem

On-orbit servicing is often too expensive because every mission is custom.

Satellites can be valuable enough to inspect, extend, relocate, or decommission — but still difficult to service economically.

When every spacecraft requires custom analysis, custom procedures, and specialized integration work, servicing becomes harder to justify. Antandros is developing technologies that reduce that custom mission burden.

Space asset management

The orbital economy needs better asset management.

As more satellites operate in crowded orbits, operators need better ways to inspect, evaluate, extend, reposition, and retire space assets.

Assess

Understand visible condition, configuration, degradation, damage, or pre-servicing state through advanced in-orbit inspection.

Extend

Support future life-extension servicing by making satellites better candidates for affordable orbital support.

Reposition

Enable future relocation or support operations through spacecraft-side serviceability planning and components.

Decommission

Support responsible end-of-life pathways as future servicing and decommissioning capabilities mature.

Credibility

Built for practical progress, not unsupported claims.

Antandros does not present current operational servicing capability. Inspection outputs are assessment-support data, and future servicing support remains a staged capability area.

The goal is practical: reduce custom mission burden, improve asset-management decisions, and make future servicing more economically worthwhile.
