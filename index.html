Making Spacecraft Service-Ready

Antandros is developing standards-aligned spacecraft readiness and servicing infrastructure for the next generation of orbital operations.

Our work begins with customer-side readiness technologies that help spacecraft prepare for future inspection, approach, docking, and servicing. Through a staged development roadmap, Antandros is also advancing toward servicer-side capabilities for non-contact inspection, servicing-readiness verification, and future physical servicing support.

What We Do

We help spacecraft teams plan for servicing-readiness.

Antandros is developing customer-side readiness technologies and standards-aligned documentation practices intended to reduce bespoke integration effort across future servicing scenarios.

In parallel, we are developing a staged roadmap toward non-contact inspection and servicing-readiness verification, with future servicing support treated as a later capability rather than a current operational service.

Core Focus Areas

Readiness technologies, inspection and assessment support, and standards alignment.

R

Readiness criteria definition

Develop readiness criteria and documentation practices that support compatibility planning and staged servicing pathways.

I

Inspection and assessment support

Advance non-contact inspection outputs intended to support engineering assessment and servicing-readiness verification.

S

Standards traceability

Map readiness assumptions and interface decisions to standards-aligned references and customer documentation.

C

Compatibility planning

Support interoperability planning for cooperative spacecraft without claiming universal compatibility outcomes.

Inspection outputs are assessment-support data and may require customer engineering review and mission-specific validation.

Why It Matters

Service-ready design can reduce future servicing friction.

Future in-space servicing is more practical when spacecraft are designed with readiness criteria, documentation discipline, and compatibility planning from the beginning.

Antandros focuses on staged, risk-aware, evidence-driven development so customers can evaluate future servicing pathways with clearer technical assumptions.
