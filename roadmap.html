Risk-reduction logic

The first mission does less so the platform can learn more.

Before contact

Prove that the servicer can approach a cooperative target, operate within conservative safety constraints, gather inspection products, and retreat without imposing contact risk.

Before capture

Establish target characterization, geometry confidence, fault responses, and abort behavior before future physical servicing support enters the roadmap.

Before future refueling support

Limit early claims to prepared-spacecraft refueling compatibility as a future pathway, not a near-term unprepared-refueling promise.

Expected outputs

A roadmap that investors, customers, and agencies can evaluate.

The immediate deliverable is not current operational servicing capability. It is a staged, evidence-driven foundation: readiness criteria, risk analysis, standards alignment, verification planning, and demonstration milestones.

This keeps the first public story credible while still showing how Antandros can expand from inspection-readiness verification into future servicing support capabilities.
