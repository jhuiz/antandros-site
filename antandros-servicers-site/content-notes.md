# Antandros content notes

## Source of truth used

The website was rebuilt around the provided Antandros SBIR-style draft. The key positioning from that draft:

- Antandros is a spacecraft-servicing company in the in-space servicing domain.
- The proposed focus is a standards-aligned **servicer-side spacecraft architecture**.
- The initial service focus is **Advanced Non-Contact Inspection and Servicing-Readiness Verification**.
- Antandros also has a separate customer-side readiness package and cooperative interface as background technology.
- The roadmap should be staged and conservative: non-contact inspection first, later contact-capable services.
- Future services may include docking support, towing, relocation, deorbit support, ORU-related servicing, and prepared-spacecraft refueling compatibility.
- The copy should not claim that Phase I builds or flies a full operational servicer spacecraft.
- The copy should not claim universal compatibility with all spacecraft.
- The copy should treat diagnostics as sensor- and customer-review-dependent.

## Public-message hierarchy

1. **Hero message:** Standards-aligned spacecraft servicing starts before contact.
2. **Company description:** Antandros is developing a servicer-side architecture for advanced non-contact inspection and servicing-readiness verification of cooperative spacecraft.
3. **Why it matters:** servicing is hard to scale when every mission requires a custom interface, custom approach procedure, custom robotic tool, and custom risk analysis.
4. **First service:** non-contact inspection and readiness verification.
5. **Roadmap:** feasibility-backed architecture → ground maturation → orbital non-contact inspection → later contact-capable services.

## Conservative claims to preserve

- “Developing” rather than “operating.”
- “Assessment-support data” rather than “full diagnostics.”
- “Prepared-spacecraft refueling compatibility” rather than “refueling any spacecraft.”
- “Standards-aware / standards-aligned / traceability” rather than “universal standard.”
- “Staged future extensions” rather than “current services.”
